How this works – read me first

What is this?

Every member of our kingdom, ranked on what they actually did this week. It reads the weekly kingdom export straight from the spreadsheet – nobody has to add anything up by hand.

How do I add a new week?

Open Weekly spreadsheets above, then drag the .xlsx export onto the box (or click Choose .xlsx files). The rankings rebuild straight away. Weeks sort themselves by the scan date inside the file, so it does not matter what order you add them in.

Uploading the same week twice replaces it rather than duplicating it, so a corrected export is safe to re-upload.

What does the score mean?

A number from 0 to 100 measuring this week’s effort against everyone else in the kingdom. It is not a measure of how big an account is – a well-run small account can outscore a whale who did nothing.

It blends how a member ranks against everyone with how large their numbers are in absolute terms, so one enormous KvK score cannot drown out the other 480 people.

What are the tiers?

Cut by position, every week:

  • S top 10% – carrying the kingdom
  • A next 15% – strong contributors
  • B and C the middle 50%
  • D next 15% – needs a nudge
  • F bottom 10% – barely showed up

What do the tags mean?

  • DEAD not logged in for 14+ days
  • INACTIVE not logged in for 7+ days
  • LOW scored under 25 this week
  • NO SUPPORT zero tech donations, helps and building time
  • NO BASELINE not in last week’s export, so their weekly gain cannot be worked out yet

What is the difference between Kill and Kill +?

Kill is their total kill points, built up over the whole game. Kill + is how much they added this week – and that is the one the ranking uses. Someone who added 20M beats someone sitting on a 100M total who added 10M. Power and Power + work the same way.

Both read 0 in the very first week, because there is no earlier week to measure the gain against. They start counting once a second week is added.

Can I change what counts?

Yes – open Scoring weights above. Raise what matters to us, set anything to 0 to drop it entirely. The percentages show each stat’s real share. Everything recalculates instantly, for everyone. Reset to defaults undoes any mess.

Are the spreadsheets kept?

Yes. Every upload is stored exactly as it came in, so a week can always be rebuilt. Download backup saves the lot – every week, every setting and every original spreadsheet – as one file. Worth doing occasionally.

How do I get a list of top members?

In Member Rankings, set the number next to Export Top Members and click it. It downloads a plain .txt file numbering the top members by score, ready to paste into Discord.

It always ranks the whole kingdom for the selected week – any search or alliance/tier/status filter on the table is ignored, so the list is never accidentally narrowed.

Something looks wrong

Click any member’s row. It opens their full scorecard – every stat, what it scored, and their week-by-week history – which usually explains the number straight away.
